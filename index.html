HOW TO USE · 3 STEPS

1

Find your Steam saves. Open Steam’s userdata folder, then YOUR_STEAM_ID\1113000\remote. Save slots are named data0001.bin, data0002.bin, etc.

[Steam folder]\userdata\[Your Steam ID]\1113000\remote\data0001.bin
2

Open your save. The tracker reads it without changing the file or game. You can also compare an older save from the same playthrough.

3

Filter by missing lines and character. Each entry suggests a trigger. Some are documented; others are estimates, as noted in the label.

Why are some entries hidden?

Internal entries without known dialogue and special or boss lines are hidden by default. Use the checkbox to show them. Some may not count toward the achievement.

Why can the line count exceed 255?

The in-game counter uses one byte and wraps after 255. The tracker counts recorded flags instead, so it continues past 255.

Do I need Cheat Engine or an account?

No. Open the site and select a copy of your save. The only external request retrieves the public line list from GitHub. Your save is never uploaded.
